What is GI?

Some Things Can Only Come From One Place.

  • Kashmir Saffron belongs to Kashmir.
  • Darjeeling Tea belongs to Darjeeling.
  • Guntur Sannam Chilli belongs to Andhra Pradesh.

What makes them special isn't just how they're made. It's where they come from.

That is the beauty of a Geographical Indication (GI): a registered name that links a product's special qualities to the place it comes from.

You can recreate a product. You cannot recreate its origin.
