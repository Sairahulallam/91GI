Why GI Exists

Protecting what's truly Indian.

So the name on your pack always matches what's inside.

For centuries, India's regions have grown products the world loves: turmeric, basmati, saffron, tea.

In 1999, India gave them the recognition they deserved. The Geographical Indications Act made each famous name a protected promise: a GI product must come from its home region.

Today, India is one of the world's leading GI nations, and its origin products are trusted across the globe.

For you, that means one thing: Darjeeling Tea is from Darjeeling. Kashmir Saffron is from Kashmir. The name is the proof.

India's GI advantage, and how Befach delivers it

A Geographical Indication (GI) certifies where a product comes from. It is granted by the Government of India after formal examination, and it ties a product's name to the region that made it famous.

A certificate alone, however, does not fill a container. Buyers need consistent quality, dependable volumes and complete documentation, shipment after shipment. That is the gap between a GI tag and a GI supply chain. Befach exists to close it.

A GI tag protects the name. A reliable supply chain protects your business.

Sourced at Origin, Not at Market

Every GI is held by a registered producer body, and only Authorised Users may trade under its name. Yet much of India's GI harvest still moves through local markets, where its origin is easily lost.

Befach works at the source. We partner with the producer organisations, cooperatives and growers behind each GI, inspect produce at origin, and keep every lot traceable from farm to shipment.

A Name the World Already Values

Champagne, Parmigiano Reggiano and Scotch whisky command a premium because their origin is protected and their quality is consistent. India's origin products are on the same path. In 2011, Darjeeling Tea became the first Indian product registered as a protected GI in the European Union.

Kashmir Saffron, Mithila Makhana, Araku Valley Coffee and Sangli Raisins hold the same promise. The advantage belongs to the buyers who bring them to market first.

Built for Your Shelf

India's GI foods are not niche luxuries. Most are everyday staples (rice, pulses, chillies, spices and jaggery) with steady demand across retail, food service and manufacturing.

Premium origin. Practical pricing.

We hold quality standards high and margins disciplined, so GI products stay commercially viable on your shelf. We work with producers all year, not only at harvest, to keep supply consistent.

Every Order Strengthens the Origin

As demand for a GI grows, more growers register as Authorised Users, supply deepens, and the name becomes harder to imitate. Your business helps keep India's origins genuine.
